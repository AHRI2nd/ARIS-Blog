## Scope

This policy applies to the **Lyrical Sync Store edition**, which processes files you select on your device. This version has no LRCLIB search, online lyrics publishing, Spotify or YouTube integration, app account, advertising, analytics, remote error reporting, or app-operated cloud synchronization. The app does not upload lyrics, audio, file paths, or editing history to the developer or a lyrics service server.

## Information handled by the app

Selected audio data and track information, lyrics, and timestamps are processed in device memory for editing and playback. Saving or exporting writes lyrics to the file you choose. Your original files remain after you close the app. If you save to a cloud-synchronized folder, that separate service may synchronize the files according to its own settings.

The app stores preferences, shortcuts, and recent-file entries on your device. The recent list may contain up to eight file paths and opening times, as well as persistent macOS file-access bookmarks. Recovery copies may contain unsaved lyrics, track information, timestamps, paths, and bookmarks. This information is not a search history sent to the developer. The app does not provide its own encryption; it relies on operating-system account and file-access protections.

## Local data deletion and retention

You can clear recent-file entries from the recent files menu. Recovery copies are removed after a successful save or when you choose to discard them at the startup recovery prompt. A storage error may prevent removal; check the app's notice. Preferences may remain until changed or local app data is removed. There is no fixed automatic expiry. Clearing recent entries or recovery copies does not delete your original files.

The developer does not store app accounts or editing data on an app-operated server. Operating systems, app Stores, and user-configured backup or synchronization services handle their data under their respective policies. Requests sent to an external lyrics service by an older version remain subject to that service's retention and deletion practices; installing the Store edition does not delete those records.

## Contact and website processing

For privacy questions, contact us at the email address above. Email addresses, messages, and attachments that you include may be processed to respond to your inquiry. You do not need to send sensitive files.
