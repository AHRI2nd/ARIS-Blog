# Lyrical Sync Store edition

Lyrical Sync Store edition is a desktop app for playing local audio while editing lyrics and timestamps, then saving or exporting your work as a file.

## Store release plans

The Store edition has not launched yet. A free release with planned worldwide availability is in preparation. The first release is planned for macOS on Apple silicon (ARM64) and Windows x64.

## Store edition scope

- Play audio selected on your device while editing lyric timestamps.
- LRCLIB lyric search is not included.
- Audio and video downloads are not included.
- A built-in app updater is not included.
